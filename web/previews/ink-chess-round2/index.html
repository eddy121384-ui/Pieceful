    How to Play

    Click a piece to select it, then click a dot to move. A red ring marks a piece you can capture.

    Drag to orbit, right-drag or two-finger drag to pan, scroll or pinch to zoom.

    Esc deselect   Ctrl+Z undo   H hint

    R red view   B black view   T top view

    Standard Xiangqi rules: the Horse can be blocked, the Elephant never crosses the river, the Cannon needs exactly one piece to jump over when capturing, the two Generals may not face each other down an open file, and a player with no legal move loses.
